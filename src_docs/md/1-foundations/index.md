# Part 1. Foundations

This part covers what every later part assumes: the vocabulary of globalization, internationalization, localization and translation; how the field got from DOS code pages to continuous delivery; and the facts about characters, locales, formatted values, plurals, scripts, text growth, culture and cost that decide whether a translation can work before a translator sees it. Each chapter teaches a decision with its reasoning and traps, ends with a worked example, and states where the sources disagree.
