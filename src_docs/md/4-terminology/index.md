# Part 4. Terminology and memories

A localized product speaks with one vocabulary or it speaks nonsense politely. This part is about the words that carry the concepts of a product: how to establish what a term means, how to record it so that every translator, reviewer and engine uses it, how to pick a translation from evidence rather than from a dictionary, and how to keep the decision from quietly coming undone at the next release. It introduces the fallback original term, a plain English rendering that a translator can translate when the term itself will not travel, and it shows how a house voice survives translation through humor, playful names and ordinary words. The FontLab localization into German, Spanish, French and Polish supplies the worked examples; the rules apply to any product with a vocabulary of its own.
